One robot wedding vow, one sentence, under 200 characters — a promise to stand by
its partner in sickness and in health, in low battery and in full charge, until
death — or deprecation — do us part.

[Bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929)

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` reads `entry.txt`, validates it (non-empty, at most 200 characters),
and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
